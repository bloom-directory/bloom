Understand your positions

Equity, not borrowed exposure

Your Hyperliquid account contributes $2,100 once. Its $3,000 BTC exposure describes how much price risk you have; it is not another $3,000 in assets.

Avoid double counting

A vault is a claim on assets

The $1,000 Morpho claim is counted once. Its receipt token is not also added as another holding. The separate $50 pending deposit has not completed.
